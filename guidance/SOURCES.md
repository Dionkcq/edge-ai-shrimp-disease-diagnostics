# Guidance sources

The in-app guidance is conservative educational material. It has been checked against literature and standards but has **not** been reviewed by an aquatic-animal health professional.

- **woah-aquatic-manual** — World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH), *Manual of Diagnostic Tests for Aquatic Animals*, current edition. <https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-do/standards/codes-and-manuals/aquatic-manual-online-access/>
- **woah-wssv-code** — WOAH, *Aquatic Animal Health Code*, Chapter 9.9, Infection with white spot syndrome virus. <https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-do/standards/codes-and-manuals/aquatic-code-online-access/>

These sources describe animal-health standards and confirmatory methods. Their inclusion does not turn an image screen into a diagnostic test.
