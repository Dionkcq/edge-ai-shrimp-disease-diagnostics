# Security and safety reporting

This is a private educational project. Report security or safety problems privately rather than opening a public issue.

Contact Dion through the team's existing private channel and include:

- the affected file, feature or model version;
- steps to reproduce the problem;
- the possible impact;
- any logs or screenshots with credentials and personal information removed.

Examples include exposed credentials, committed personal data, unsafe treatment wording, a way to bypass the uncertainty gate, or a model result that is presented as a confirmed diagnosis.

Do not upload raw farm photographs, EXIF/GPS metadata, access tokens or private Drive links to an issue. The team will acknowledge the report, assess the risk and document the fix in the appropriate private channel.
