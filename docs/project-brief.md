# Original Project Brief

## Scenario

Shrimp aquaculture is vulnerable to rapid disease outbreaks. The proposed system should help small and remote farms identify visible warning signs quickly, operate without reliable internet, and provide sustainable next-step guidance.

## Required capabilities

1. **Offline edge operation** on a ruggedized mobile device or waterproof camera unit.
2. **Computer-vision analysis** using a course fine-tuned model.
3. **Visible marker screening** for signs such as shell spotting, tail discoloration and gill abnormalities.
4. **Conversational guidance** covering sustainable, approved and food-safe interventions, including water-quality adjustments and suitable probiotic practices.
5. **Risk mitigation** through rapid action and appropriate escalation.

## Corrections and interpretation

The supplied document's heading incorrectly says “Organic Weed Management and Crop Health Monitoring”; the content is about shrimp disease diagnostics.

The system must be presented as screening/decision support. An image-based prediction alone cannot definitively confirm pathogens such as WSSV or AHPND/EMS.
